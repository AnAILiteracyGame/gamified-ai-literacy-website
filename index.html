Shooting the Statement

Read the statement. Take aim. Shoot the right answer.

How to play

  1. A statement or question appears at the top of the screen.
  2. The answer choices below it are your targets.
  3. Click a target — or press 1 2 3 4 — to shoot it.
  4. You get an explanation after every shot, right or wrong.
  5. Press Enter or Space to move to the next question.
